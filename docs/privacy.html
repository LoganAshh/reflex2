Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Reflex is designed to keep your personal tracking information on your device. Reflex currently does not operate user accounts, display advertising, sell personal information, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

Information you add to Reflex

You may add a profile name and photo, habits, goals, logs, notes, cues, locations, replacement actions, review choices, and reminder settings. This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to a Reflex server.

Photos and notifications

If you choose a profile photo, Reflex uses the photo you select and saves a copy within the app's local storage. If you enable reminders, Reflex uses your device's notification system to schedule them locally. Reflex does not use remote push-notification tokens to collect your tracking information.

Exports and backups

When you choose to export your data, Reflex creates a backup file and opens your device's sharing controls. You decide where that file is sent or stored. Copies outside Reflex are controlled by you and by any service you choose to use.

Data sharing and selling

Reflex currently does not sell, rent, or share your habit-tracking information with advertisers or data brokers. Apple and your device may process limited technical information under their own terms when you download or use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you change it, use Reset all data in Reflex, or remove the app. Resetting or deleting Reflex does not delete backup files you previously exported elsewhere.

Security

Reflex relies on the security protections provided by your device and operating system. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so protect access to your device and any exported backups.

Children's privacy

Reflex is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Reflex changes. If advertising, analytics, accounts, or other services change how information is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those changes are released. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to reflexapp.support@gmail.com.
